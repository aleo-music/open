ALEO Inbox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

ALEO Inbox is a private tool operated by A.L.E.O. (Mania Records) for managing direct messages sent to the Instagram account @aleo.music. It is not offered to the public, and it does not let anyone other than the account owner log in.

What information it processes

When someone sends a direct message or reaction to @aleo.music, Meta delivers it to ALEO Inbox through the Instagram Platform. ALEO Inbox processes:

  • The sender's Instagram-scoped ID, name and username
  • The content of the message (text, or a note that it was a photo, video, voice note, shared post or reaction)
  • The time it was sent, and recent messages in the same conversation

How it is used

The information is used only to notify the account owner of new messages and to prepare suggested replies, which may be drafted with the help of an AI model (Anthropic's Claude). A reply is only ever sent when the account owner personally approves it. The information is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, except the service providers needed to run the tool (Meta, which delivers the messages, and Anthropic, which processes text to suggest replies), or when required by law.

Storage and retention

Message data is stored on a private computer controlled by the account owner, protected by access controls. It is kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation, and is deleted on request.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask for any data about you to be deleted by emailing aleo@mania-records.com with your Instagram username. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also stop all processing at any time by not messaging @aleo.music, or by blocking the account on Instagram.
